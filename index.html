League rules

The quick version.

Dealing a hand

  1. Pick a dealer. Shuffle, and everyone draws one card. Highest number deals. Skip-Bo cards don't count, so draw again.
  2. Deal the stock piles. Deal one card at a time, face down, around the table, until everyone has 30 cards, or 20 with 5 or more players. Don't look at them.
  3. Flip the top card. Everyone squares up their stock pile and turns its top card face up on top. That's the only stock card you can play.
  4. Make the draw pile. The rest of the deck goes face down in the middle of the table.
  5. Leave room. Keep space in the middle for 4 building piles, and space in front of each player for 4 discard piles. All start empty.
  6. Start play. The player to the dealer's left draws 5 cards and goes first. Play goes clockwise.

Next hand, the deal passes to the left. Short on time? Deal 10-card stock piles and set the scorekeeper to 10 cards.

Your piles

  1. SStock pile: face down, top card face up. Empty it first to win the hand. 30 cards each, or 20 with 5+ players.
  2. HHand: draw up to 5 cards at the start of each turn.
  3. BBuilding piles: 4 shared piles in the middle. Start with a 1 and go up in order to 12, then clear the pile.
  4. DDiscard piles: 4 of your own. Your turn ends when you discard one card.
  • Play from your stock pile, hand, or the top of a discard pile onto any building pile.
  • Play all 5 cards from your hand? Draw 5 more and keep going.
  • Skip-Bo cards are wild: play them as any number, including a 1 to start a pile.

Scoring

Winning the hand25
Each card left in an opponent's stock pile5
Everyone else0
  • Only the hand winner scores.
  • Example: opponents have 6 and 9 cards left. The winner scores 25 + 5 × 15 = 100.
  • First player to 500 wins the game.
  • Partners: play from your own and your partner's stock and discard piles.
